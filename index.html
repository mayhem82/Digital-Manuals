Processing facility — design baseline

Custom-built, not a standard shipping container conversion.

Required internal clear zone lengths total 12.192 m. Structural and partition allowances are additional to the clear lengths.

Dimensioned zoning diagram, not fabrication CAD. Equipment positions remain preliminary.

Internal clearances

Baseline pre-insulation internal width 2.350–2.380 m; resulting clear width A 2.200–2.230 m, B/C 2.250–2.280 m.

Baseline pre-insulation internal height 2.690 m; ceiling-insulated height A 2.615 m and B/C 2.640 m, before floor and service build-up. Shell must be engineered around required clearances.

Zone A — Cold storage

75 mm insulation; plug-in freezers Phase 1; future cold room; 900–1000 mm viewing-window door; pressure relief port; coved floor; future refrigerant conduits; no Phase 1 floor drain.

Zone B — Processing

50 mm insulation; side entrance and fixed double-glazed window; positive pressure; stainless benches, heated handwash, double sink, dishwasher, filtered water; strained wet-processing drain and approved wastewater connection.

Zone C — Packaging and office

50 mm insulation; packaging bench, storage, office nook and fixed window; insulated internal door behind exterior double doors.

Engineering dependencies

JIS SS400-equivalent documented steel, reinforced openings, transport and seismic restraints, 60 Hz 200 V equipment / 100 V office circuits, RCDs, grounding, HVAC, wastewater, washable flooring, access/cranage and site loads require engineering and authority review.
