Organise your home, help and know-how.

Make a trusted trades contact list

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Notebook
  • recommendations

Steps

  1. List a plumber, electrician, roofer and equipment technician.
  2. Check relevant licensing and confirm the work they undertake.
  3. Record normal and after-hours contacts beside your property notes.

Know the limit: Get a clear scope and price process; never share access codes publicly.

Describe a problem clearly

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Phone camera
  • model details

Steps

  1. Write what changed, when it began and any warning indicators.
  2. Take photos only from a safe accessible position.
  3. Give the technician model information and access needs before booking.

Know the limit: Do not recreate a dangerous fault just to film it.

Organise appliance manuals

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Folder or local digital files

Steps

  1. Record model and serial numbers from safe visible labels.
  2. Save manufacturer manuals and service records together.
  3. Add reminders for user checks and professional servicing.

Know the limit: A similar-looking model can have different instructions.

Create a home information folder

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Folder
  • secure storage

Steps

  1. Include property plan, utility providers and repair contacts.
  2. Add insurance and important document copies securely.
  3. Tell a trusted person where the folder is, by agreement.

Know the limit: Limit sensitive information and share only what is needed.

Plan to work alone safely

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Check-in partner
  • charged phone

Steps

  1. Choose a low-risk task and check conditions and exits.
  2. Tell someone where you are and when to expect a check-in.
  3. Stop when conditions change or the task exceeds your ability.

Know the limit: A phone is not a substitute for a second person on a hazardous task.

Learn a task with a trusted helper

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Simple task
  • suitable tools

Steps

  1. Ask for a demonstration with explanations and safety limits.
  2. Practise the safe parts under supervision.
  3. Write your own steps and know when to stop and ask for help.

Know the limit: Nobody needs to prove independence by attempting a risky task.

Prepare for a service visit

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Work description
  • access plan

Steps

  1. Confirm who is attending, the purpose and the arrival window.
  2. Arrange safe access and ask about pets, power interruptions and mobility needs.
  3. Keep a record of work and any follow-up instructions.

Know the limit: Verify unexpected visitors before giving access.

Make a seasonal confidence plan

Skill area: Everyday independence. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • This skill library
  • calendar

Steps

  1. Choose one useful low-risk skill and one preparedness task.
  2. Schedule time with the right manual or helper.
  3. Record what you learnt and the next step you can safely attempt.

Know the limit: Progress comes from practice and knowing your limits.
