SET IT UP IN A QUIET MOMENT

Your phone & radio checklist.

Print this guide

Menus and availability depend on your device and software. Review the current official instructions. Never place a test emergency call.

01

Local radio, ready to go

  1. Find the property local ABC AM/FM station with the frequency finder.
  2. Tune and preset a battery radio and car radio in normal conditions.
  3. Write the frequency in your plan and keep spare batteries. ABC listen streaming needs internet.

02

Hazards Near Me NSW

  1. Install from the official NSW Government download links.
  2. Create watch zones home, work and other places that matter to you.
  3. Allow app notifications and location access the features you use. Check phone notification permissions; use other warning channels too.

03

AusAlert emergency broadcasts

  1. Update your device and check official compatibility. No app or signup is.
  2. iPhone: Settings → Notifications → AusAlert → keep Priority AusAlert on.
  3. Android: Settings → Safety and Emergency → Wireless Emergency Alerts → keep Priority AusAlert on. Names vary. Critical Alerts cannot be through these settings.

04

iPhone SOS & Medical ID

  1. Settings → Emergency SOS: review Call with Hold and Release and Call with 5 Button Presses.
  2. Health → profile picture → Medical ID: add chosen contacts and useful details.
  3. Decide whether Show When Locked is right you; it reveals those details to someone holding the phone. Read controls without placing a test call.

05

Android & Samsung SOS

  1. On supported phones: Settings → Safety & emergency → Emergency SOS → setup.
  2. Check 000 is the emergency number, add chosen contacts and review feature permissions.
  3. Review medical information and lock-screen access. Samsung has countdown and confirmation options. Features vary by device; never test with a real emergency call.

06

Location & no-coverage planning

  1. Install Emergency+ and permit location access. It can show location details to read to an operator; it does not create reception.
  2. 112 reaches the same emergency service as 000. It does not create satellite coverage. No network coverage means ordinary mobile calls cannot connect.
  3. Supported iPhone 14+ models in Australia may offer satellite SOS outdoors with clear sky. Check Apple requirements and use the built-in demo before travel.

Telephone warning messages

Emergency Alert can send recorded landline calls and SMS a warning area. Keep provider address details current. A switched-off phone, no reception or power loss can prevent delivery; NBN landlines may fail during an outage.

NSW RFS telephone warnings

Accessible emergency calls

Ordinary SMS to 000 or 106 is not supported. 106 is the TTY emergency service. People who are Deaf, hard of hearing or have speech communication difficulty can prepare an official National Relay Service emergency option.

NRS emergency options and SMS Relay

UHF on country properties

Learn the owner agreed channel and call signs. UHF CB emergency channels 5/35 do not guarantee rescue and are not a substitute 000. Emergency services do not routinely monitor them.

ACMA CB radio guidance

Give a clear emergency report

Say FIRE, POLICE or AMBULANCE; give the town, street address and correct gate. Explain what happened and who needs help, including access or communication needs. Stay on the line and follow the operator.
