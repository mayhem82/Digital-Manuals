Tools & fixings

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

Know the tool. Learn the technique.

Build a basic toolkit

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • A small sturdy toolbox

Steps

  1. Start with a tape, screwdrivers, pliers, adjustable spanner and torch.
  2. Add eye protection, suitable gloves and a small hammer.
  3. Label a home for each tool and check condition before use.

Know the limit: Buy for the task and your grip; powered equipment needs separate instruction.

Choose the right screwdriver

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Screwdrivers or bit set

Steps

  1. Identify the screw head: slotted, Phillips, Pozidriv or Torx.
  2. Match type and size so the bit seats fully.
  3. Press in line with the screw and turn slowly.

Know the limit: Stop if the bit slips; never use a screwdriver as a pry bar.

Use a spanner without slipping

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Correct-size spanner

Steps

  1. Match the tool to the nut; set adjustable jaws snugly.
  2. Keep fingers clear and support the item.
  3. Apply steady force within the tool rating; reposition as needed.

Know the limit: Do not extend a handle or work on gas, pressurised or electrical fittings.

Clamp before working

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Suitable clamps
  • scrap pads

Steps

  1. Put the work on a stable bench with space around it.
  2. Use pads to protect the surface and clamp securely.
  3. Check nothing can rotate or fall before starting.

Know the limit: Keep hands outside the work path; clamps do not replace guards.

Learn a cordless drill on scrap

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Drill manual
  • scrap timber
  • eye protection

Steps

  1. Read the controls and practise with the battery removed first.
  2. Secure scrap timber and fit the correct bit to instructions.
  3. Use low speed and an appropriate clutch setting for practice screws.

Know the limit: Do not drill walls until hidden services and material hazards are assessed.

Select a fixing for the material

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Packaging
  • sample material

Steps

  1. Identify timber, plasterboard, masonry or another substrate.
  2. Check the fixing load rating and supported installation method.
  3. Follow its instructions and verify the finished attachment.

Know the limit: A wall scanner cannot guarantee no hidden services; heavy loads need expert advice.

Look after hand tools

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Brush
  • dry cloth
  • suitable storage

Steps

  1. Remove debris after use and dry tools before storing.
  2. Inspect handles, jaws and cutting edges for damage.
  3. Remove damaged tools from use and store sharp edges protected.

Know the limit: Do not grind or repair damaged tools without the appropriate skill.

Decide when a job needs training

Skill area: Tools & fixings. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Task manual
  • qualified helper

Steps

  1. List hazards: height, electricity, moving parts, pressure and dust.
  2. Check whether training, licensing or specialised equipment is needed.
  3. Book instruction or a professional before attempting the job.

Know the limit: Chainsaws, machinery, confined spaces and asbestos are not beginner practice tasks.
