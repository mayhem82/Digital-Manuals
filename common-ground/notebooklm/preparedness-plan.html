Personal or neighbour preparedness plan and emergency kit

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

Make the plan together and use only the details the resident wants recorded. Keep private access and medical details secure. A personal plan saved in the interactive website stays in that browser; this source contains a blank planning framework.

Planning fields

  • Plan for (household or agreed neighbour support)
  • Trusted contact & backup helper
  • Preferred communication & no-response plan
  • Local radio station & AM/FM frequency
  • Leave-early triggers & warning sources
  • Accessible destination & backup
  • Transport, mobility aids & equipment
  • Animals: care, transport & destination
  • Water, gates, utilities & service contacts
  • Agreed neighbour tasks & review date

Emergency essentials checklist

  • Drinking water and suitable food
  • Medication, prescriptions and personal-care needs
  • Torch, battery radio and spare batteries
  • Charged devices, cables and power banks
  • First-aid kit and relevant training
  • Document copies, cash and spare keys
  • Suitable clothing, footwear and hygiene items
  • Mobility / communication aids and backup needs
  • Animal food, water, leads, carriers and records
  • Printed plan, local maps and contact numbers
  • Safe transport and an accessible destination
  • A backup helper and agreed check-in plan
  • NSW SES emergency kit
  • Use the interactive plan
