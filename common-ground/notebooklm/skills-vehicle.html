Everyday checks and safer journeys.

Check tyre pressure

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Tyre gauge
  • vehicle manual

Steps

  1. Find the recommended pressures on the placard or manual.
  2. Measure when tyres are cold as the manufacturer specifies.
  3. Adjust to the correct setting and replace valve caps.

Know the limit: Do not use the sidewall maximum as your target; inspect damaged tyres professionally.

  • Vehicle maintenance guidance

Check oil and fluid indicators

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Vehicle manual
  • cloth

Steps

  1. Park safely on level ground and follow the manual for engine state.
  2. Read the specified dipstick or electronic oil display.
  3. Use only the specified fluid and seek help if levels keep falling.

Know the limit: Never open a hot cooling system; hybrids and EVs have separate hazards.

  • Vehicle maintenance guidance

Know your flat-tyre options

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Manual
  • roadside assistance details

Steps

  1. Locate your spare or repair kit and check its condition.
  2. Read vehicle-specific lifting and temporary tyre restrictions before travel.
  3. If roadside conditions are unsafe, move clear and call assistance.

Know the limit: Never get under a vehicle supported by a jack or change a wheel beside moving traffic.

  • Vehicle maintenance guidance

Check lights before a journey

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Helper or reflective surface

Steps

  1. Park securely and operate headlights, indicators and hazard lights.
  2. Check brake lights with an agreed helper standing safely clear.
  3. Arrange repair for faults before a trip.

Know the limit: Use the manual for bulb replacement; sealed units may need a technician.

  • Vehicle maintenance guidance

Prepare for a rural drive

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Paper map
  • water
  • charged phone

Steps

  1. Check fuel or charge, route, weather and road closures.
  2. Share the route, destination and overdue check-in time.
  3. Carry water and backup navigation for areas without reception.

Know the limit: Never enter floodwater or a closed road.

Handle a breakdown safely

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Roadside assistance number

Steps

  1. Pull to a safer location if possible and use hazard lights.
  2. Assess traffic before leaving the vehicle; move to a protected area if safe.
  3. Give assistance your location, vehicle and any accessibility needs.

Know the limit: Call 000 for immediate danger; do not attempt repairs in a traffic lane.

Plan a trailer lesson

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Trailer manual
  • qualified instructor

Steps

  1. Identify tow ratings, couplings, load limits and required checks.
  2. Arrange supervised instruction for hitching, reversing and load restraint.
  3. Practise in a suitable clear area before a real journey.

Know the limit: Do not tow until compatibility, brakes and legal requirements are confirmed.

Keep a vehicle emergency kit

Skill area: Vehicles & getting around. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Secure storage box

Steps

  1. Pack suitable water, torch, phone cable, first-aid kit and weather protection.
  2. Add written contacts and medication needs relevant to the trip.
  3. Secure the kit so it cannot become a projectile.

Know the limit: Heat affects food, batteries and medication; review storage instructions.

  • NSW SES emergency kit
