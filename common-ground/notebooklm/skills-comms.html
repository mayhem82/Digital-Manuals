Set up Hazards Near Me NSW

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Compatible phone
  • internet

Steps

  1. Install the app from the NSW Government links.
  2. Create watch zones for home and places that matter to you.
  3. Allow notifications and review location settings for the features you use.

Know the limit: Use radio and official websites too; notifications are not guaranteed.

Find your local emergency radio

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Battery or car radio

Steps

  1. Use the ABC frequency finder for the property location.
  2. Tune and test the station in normal conditions.
  3. Write the frequency on a paper plan and keep spare batteries.

Know the limit: Online radio streaming needs an internet connection.

Give an emergency location

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Property address
  • gate directions

Steps

  1. Record the street address, town, entrance and landmarks.
  2. Check coordinates in a suitable mapping or Emergency+ app.
  3. In an emergency, say the location clearly and follow operator questions.

Know the limit: Do not rely on a map pin being accurate or mobile coverage being available.

Check phone emergency settings

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Your phone
  • official device instructions

Steps

  1. Review SOS controls without triggering a call.
  2. Add agreed emergency contacts and consider lock-screen medical information.
  3. Check warning notifications and practise only supported demo modes.

Know the limit: Never make a test call to 000; settings differ by device and software.

Use a check-in arrangement

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Trusted person
  • written agreement

Steps

  1. Agree check-in times and the preferred communication method.
  2. Specify what to do if there is no response and who is the backup.
  3. Review after travel, weather changes or support changes.

Know the limit: Silence can be a missed message; agree a clear escalation plan in advance.

Choose the right help number

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Printed contact list

Steps

  1. Use 000 for fire or immediate life-threatening danger.
  2. Call NSW SES 132 500 for flood, storm or tsunami emergency help.
  3. Use provider and non-urgent service contacts for other issues.

Know the limit: Do not treat an information line as an emergency dispatch service.

Plan communication without coverage

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Paper plan
  • backup device advice

Steps

  1. Identify reception gaps before working or travelling.
  2. Agree a route, check-in time and overdue response.
  3. Consider a registered beacon or suitable satellite service for remote activities.

Know the limit: 112 does not create satellite coverage; UHF radio does not guarantee rescue.

Make communication accessible

Skill area: Communication & help. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Person preferred method
  • paper notes

Steps

  1. Ask whether speech, text, large print, relay or another method works best.
  2. Record agreed help options and location details in an accessible format.
  3. Practise the plan together without making live emergency calls.

Know the limit: Ordinary SMS to 000 or 106 is not supported; use official NRS options.
