ABOUT COMMON GROUND

Know-how belongs to everyone.

A practical NSW field guide women learning independently, country newcomers, community helpers and anyone filling a gap in their skills.

Start where you are.

Skills are learned, practised and passed on. This guide brings together everyday maintenance, farm resourcefulness, emergency preparation and person-led neighbour support. It makes no assumptions about a person’s ability based on gender, age or disability.

How to use the guide

There are 104 starter guides across 13 areas. They introduce tasks and safe decision points. Use the exact manufacturer instructions and suitable hands-on training your equipment. Licensing and site requirements vary; use qualified trades where.

Emergency information

The seven scenario guides cover common NSW preparation and recovery needs. They cannot cover every property, support need or changing incident. Current warnings and emergency-service directions take priority. In immediate danger, fire or a life-threatening emergency call 000; NSW flood and storm emergency help call 132 500.

Your information

Saved guides, checklists, personal plans and survey drafts use this browser’s local storage. They are not synced across devices and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Do not store private access or medical details on a shared device.

Only an intentionally submitted survey goes into the shared database: confidence answers, workshop choices, optional broad area,s, session times, participation needs and property features, plus a random response ID and submission time. The results page shows aggregate counts. Them asks nos, exact addresses or contact information. It uses no advertising trackers. Hosting services may process technical connection information under their own terms.

The surveym does not verifyentities: counts are submitted responses, not verified unique people. Device-local duplicate protection limits accidental repeats. Use results with local consultation, accessible recruitment and qualified trainers.
