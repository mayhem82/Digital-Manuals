Garden & food

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

Useful habits outside and in the kitchen.

Water a garden efficiently

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Soil check
  • watering equipment

Steps

  1. Check soil below the surface before adding water.
  2. Water the root zone when conditions and local restrictions allow.
  3. Adjust after rain and repair visible hose leaks.

Know the limit: Keep hoses clear of access paths and distinguish non-potable water.

Connect a hose without a leak

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Matching hose fittings

Steps

  1. Turn the tap off and inspect the washer and connector.
  2. Fit compatible parts by hand without cross-threading.
  3. Turn on slowly and check seals; turn off after use.

Know the limit: Do not attach garden hoses to drinking systems without suitable backflow protection.

Use hand pruners safely

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Suitable pruners
  • gloves
  • eye protection

Steps

  1. Identify the plant and choose small reachable growth.
  2. Keep the free hand clear and cut within tool capacity.
  3. Clean and lock the tool before storage.

Know the limit: Overhead, large or storm-damaged branches need an arborist.

Start a small compost bin

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Suitable bin
  • dry and green garden material

Steps

  1. Choose an accessible location clear of essential paths.
  2. Add suitable plant scraps and dry garden matter as the bin guide recommends.
  3. Manage moisture and pests; wash hands afterwards.

Know the limit: Avoid breathing dust; follow product warnings for compost and potting mix.

Plan a low-maintenance garden

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Paper plan
  • owner priorities

Steps

  1. Mark sun, water access and safe paths.
  2. Choose manageable beds and locally suitable plants.
  3. Keep working areas reachable without leaning from unstable positions.

Know the limit: Ask about allergies, sensory needs and mobility before changing a shared garden.

Store food for an outage

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Thermometer
  • shelf-stable supplies

Steps

  1. Keep suitable shelf-stable food and a manual can opener.
  2. Record fridge and freezer food-safety guidance from NSW Food Authority.
  3. During an outage minimise door opening and assess food against official advice.

Know the limit: Smell alone cannot establish that food is safe.

Label household chemicals

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Original containers
  • secure storage

Steps

  1. Keep each product in its original labelled container.
  2. Read storage instructions and separate incompatible products.
  3. Arrange approved disposal for unknown or damaged containers.

Know the limit: Never mix cleaning chemicals or decant them into drink bottles.

Make a seasonal outdoor checklist

Skill area: Garden & food. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Notebook
  • calendar

Steps

  1. List hoses, drainage, garden access and outdoor furniture.
  2. Set inspections before storm and fire seasons.
  3. Complete low-risk tasks and book contractors early.

Know the limit: Do ground-level checks only when conditions are safe.
