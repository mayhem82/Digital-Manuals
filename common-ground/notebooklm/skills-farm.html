Understand gravity-fed water

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • Property sketch
  • tank information

Steps

  1. Find the vertical drop from the water surface to the outlet.
  2. Estimate static head at about 9.8 kPa per metre of drop.
  3. Check the required flow and operating pressure with a suitable designer.

Know the limit: As water level falls, head falls. Pipes and fittings reduce pressure while flowing.

Sketch a gravity water system

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • Paper
  • owner knowledge

Steps

  1. Mark the source, high and low points, outlets and isolation valves.
  2. Record pipe routes, approximate distances and known equipment.
  3. Use the sketch to discuss suitable gravity uses with a water-system adviser.

Know the limit: Stock watering or garden flow does not prove firefighting performance.

Check a raised tank arrangement

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • Manufacturer details
  • qualified adviser

Steps

  1. Record tank capacity, support information and visible damage from the ground.
  2. Ask about the base, stand, anchorage, access and local approval requirements.
  3. Use a stand or base designed for the tank, load and site.

Know the limit: 1,000 litres of water weighs about one tonne, before the tank and stand. Never improvise structural supports.

Assess water for fire protection

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • System records
  • RFS or qualified designer

Steps

  1. Record the reserve, outlets and firefighter access.
  2. Have required flow, pressure, duration, materials and connections assessed.
  3. Ask your local RFS Fire Control Centre about suitable static water supply identification.

Know the limit: A gravity garden supply is not a certified suppression system. Keep a leave-early plan.

Check a trough float from a safe position

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • Owner demonstration
  • notebook

Steps

  1. Ask the owner how the float controls the normal water level.
  2. Observe whether filling stops and note overflow or lack of water.
  3. Report faults and arrange an experienced person to isolate or repair.

Know the limit: Do not enter with unfamiliar stock or dismantle pressurised fittings.

Make an inventory of reusable materials

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • Owner agreement
  • labelled storage

Steps

  1. List sound spare fittings, timber and hand tools already available.
  2. Keep useful parts dry, sorted and labelled with their purpose.
  3. Match materials to a suitable low-risk project after checking condition.

Know the limit: Unknown, treated or asbestos-containing material needs specialist advice; salvaged parts are not automatically load-rated.

Keep a useful water-fittings kit

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • System details
  • matching spare parts

Steps

  1. Record connector types and sizes without disconnecting working plumbing.
  2. Keep compatible washers, caps and spare user-serviceable parts.
  3. Ask an experienced helper to show safe low-pressure garden-hose changes.

Know the limit: A licensed plumber must assess mains connections and backflow protection.

Capture a farmer’s useful tricks

Skill area: Farm know-how & resourcefulness. Level: Learn with a local.

Have to hand

  • Notebook
  • willing local mentor

Steps

  1. Ask what problem the idea solves and when it should not be used.
  2. Record materials, maintenance, limitations and the right person to call.
  3. Practise an agreed low-risk task together and add a clear labelled sketch.

Know the limit: Pass on safety limits as carefully as the trick itself.
