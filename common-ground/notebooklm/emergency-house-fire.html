House fire

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

A house fire requires immediate escape. Practise your plan before you need it.

Urgent action: Get out, stay out and call 000. Never re-enter; tell firefighters if someone is missing.

Before

  1. Maintain working smoke alarms and arrange help with maintenance if needed.
  2. Plan usable exits and a meeting place.
  3. Agree assistance for everyone in the household.
  4. Practise without blocking exits or triggering live emergency calls.

During

  1. Leave immediately and stay low under smoke.
  2. Use stairs rather than lifts where applicable.
  3. Once outside, stay outside and call 000.
  4. Tell firefighters about missing people or animals; do not go back inside.

After

  1. Wait for fire-service permission before entering.
  2. Have structural, electrical and equipment damage assessed.
  3. Use alternative accommodation if the home is unsafe.
  4. Review escape and support arrangements before returning.

Country properties

Keep access and address identification clear so responders can find the correct entrance.
