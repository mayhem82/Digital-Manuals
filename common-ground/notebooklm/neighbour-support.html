Agreed property walk-through tasks

Gates, ramps & access

Agree which paths and entrances must stay clear. Check visible address and gate directions.

Tanks, bore & pumps

Map drinking-water supply, safe level checks, pump dependence and service contacts.

Raised / gravity-fed tanks

Record tank and outlet heights; have stand safety and intended pressure/flow assessed.

Power-dependent equipment

Let the person and their provider set backup duration, relocation triggers and equipment transport.

Septic / wastewater

Record the service contact, alarms and power needs. Keep service access clear.

Pets or livestock

Agree feeding, water, safe handling, backup carers and early relocation.

Sheds, tools & fuel

Identify safe access and who handles machinery, chemicals, damaged structures and fuel.

Phone, radio & warnings

Set up agreed watch zones, test radio reception and record preferred communication.
