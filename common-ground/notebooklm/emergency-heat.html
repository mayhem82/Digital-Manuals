Extreme heat

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

Plan a cooler place and agreed check-ins, especially if home cooling depends on electricity.

Urgent action: Confusion, collapse or seizures can indicate heatstroke. Call 000 and follow dispatcher advice while cooling with cool water or wet cloths.

Before

  1. Identify a cool place and transport that meets your needs.
  2. Plan check-ins with people who want support.
  3. Ask your provider about medication storage and medical equipment.
  4. Follow clinician advice if you have a fluid restriction.

During

  1. Stay cool and reduce physical exertion.
  2. Drink water regularly unless your clinician has given different instructions.
  3. Use agreed check-ins and move to a suitable cool place early.
  4. Call 000 for signs of heatstroke; follow dispatcher instructions.

After

  1. Seek medical advice if symptoms persist or worsen.
  2. Check food and medicine storage after heat or outages.
  3. Rest and review what made staying cool difficult.
  4. Update the plan and backup destination together.

Country properties

Outdoor work, stock care and distance to cool public spaces need early planning. Avoid strenuous property tasks in extreme heat.
