Power & communication outage

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

Know which equipment stops and how long your backup will last.

Urgent action: Call 000 if a loss of essential medical support creates immediate danger. Never run a fuel-burning generator indoors.

Before

  1. Ask equipment providers about safe backup time and alternative arrangements.
  2. Keep torches, radio, charged power banks and paper contacts ready.
  3. Agree early relocation, accessible transport and a backup helper.
  4. Check provider life-support arrangements where relevant; supply is not guaranteed.

During

  1. Use battery lighting and conserve phone power.
  2. Follow your equipment provider backup plan.
  3. Run generators only outdoors, away from openings, with compliant connections.
  4. Check agreed neighbours by the planned method; avoid fallen wires.

After

  1. Check for equipment faults before resuming normal use.
  2. Use NSW Food Authority advice to assess fridge and freezer contents.
  3. Recharge backups and replace depleted supplies.
  4. Review the plan with the person and their providers.

Country properties

Electric pumps, bore water, stock water, gates and NBN phones may all stop together. Include each in your backup plan.
