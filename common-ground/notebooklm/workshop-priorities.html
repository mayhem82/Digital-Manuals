How workshop priorities are selected

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

The live site summarises intentionally submitted surveys. Results are self-reported responses, not proof of ability or a representative population survey.

Topics are sorted first by workshop requests, then by Need to learn answers, then by Some practice answers. Confidence counts include Not relevant. Workshop interest is a separate choice.

Organisers can compare participation needs, times, property features and broad NSW areas, make a device-local shortlist, print it and download a summary CSV. Suggested session types are planning ideas. No workshops, dates or trainers have been booked through this site.

This study source describes the method. It does not contain individual submissions or a live snapshot of community response totals.
