High winds & severe storms

Plain HTML study copy for importing into NotebookLM or another reading tool. Current official warnings and emergency-service directions take priority.

Do the securing and maintenance before the weather arrives. Shelter when it does.

Urgent action: Stay indoors away from windows. Assume fallen powerlines, and anything touching them, are live.

Before

  1. Secure or store loose tools, furniture and other outdoor items.
  2. Arrange professional inspection of vulnerable roofs and trees in good weather.
  3. Charge devices, prepare battery lighting and identify suitable shelter.
  4. Check BOM and NSW SES warnings, including flood and coastal hazards.

During

  1. Shelter indoors away from windows and avoid unnecessary travel.
  2. Keep away from trees, waterways and powerlines.
  3. Follow official evacuation or shelter instructions for your location.
  4. Do not climb onto a roof or go out to retrieve loose items.

After

  1. Keep clear of fallen powerlines and objects touching them.
  2. Watch for hanging branches, damaged roofing and unstable structures.
  3. Call NSW SES 132 500 for storm emergency help; use 000 for immediate danger.
  4. Arrange qualified repairs and insurer advice for permanent damage.

Country properties

Wind can disable gates, pumps and communications. Include an accessible indoor shelter and backup animal care.
