Understand your setup and its limits.

Identify your switchboard safely

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Torch
  • electrician contact

Steps

  1. Locate the closed switchboard and read visible labels.
  2. Photograph the labels from a safe position for your records.
  3. Ask a licensed electrician to explain isolation and safety switches.

Know the limit: Do not open covers, touch exposed parts or attempt electrical repairs.

  • Electrical Safety Office: use a licensed electrician

Respond to a power outage

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Battery torch
  • charged phone

Steps

  1. Check the distributor outage service using mobile data if available.
  2. Use battery lighting and conserve device power.
  3. Follow your equipment backup plan and check on agreed neighbours.

Know the limit: Treat fallen wires as live; urgent medical backup failures need 000.

  • NSW power outages

Check leads and power boards

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Good lighting

Steps

  1. Switch off and unplug before a visual inspection.
  2. Look for heat marks, damaged insulation or loose sockets.
  3. Remove damaged items from use and replace or have them assessed.

Know the limit: Never repair mains wiring or run extension leads through floodwater.

  • Electrical Safety Office: use a licensed electrician

Plan for power-dependent equipment

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Equipment provider
  • support person

Steps

  1. List what needs power: mobility aids, beds, pumps and medical devices.
  2. Ask providers about backup duration and safe alternatives.
  3. Write an early relocation trigger and accessible destination.

Know the limit: Life-support registration does not guarantee uninterrupted electricity.

  • NSW power outages

Understand generator safety

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Generator manual
  • electrician

Steps

  1. Plan a dry outdoor location well away from openings.
  2. Arrange compliant connections with a licensed electrician.
  3. Follow the manual for fuel, loading and cooling before refuelling.

Know the limit: Never run indoors, in a garage or near windows; never backfeed a house.

  • NSW Health carbon monoxide safety

Record solar and battery contacts

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Installer documents

Steps

  1. Locate shutdown instructions supplied by the installer.
  2. Keep service details and system labels in your emergency folder.
  3. Arrange a technician explanation of what remains energised.

Know the limit: Solar and batteries can remain live when mains power is off.

  • Electrical Safety Office: use a licensed electrician

Recognise a possible gas leak

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Gas emergency contact

Steps

  1. Move away from the smell and avoid flames or electrical switching.
  2. Get people clear and call the gas emergency service from a safe place.
  3. Call 000 if there is immediate danger.

Know the limit: Do not search for leaks with a flame, adjust regulators or repair gas fittings.

Make a safe lighting backup

Skill area: Power & utilities. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Battery torches
  • batteries

Steps

  1. Put a torch by the bed and another in the emergency kit.
  2. Check batteries and keep compatible spares.
  3. Show household members where lighting is stored.

Know the limit: Keep torches accessible; candles create an extra fire risk.

  • NSW SES emergency kit
