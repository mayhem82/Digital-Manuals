Plan ahead for the lives in your care.

Make a pet evacuation kit

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Carrier
  • lead
  • pet records

Steps

  1. Pack food, water, bowls, medication instructions and identification.
  2. Check carrier fit and practise calm entry ahead of time.
  3. Choose a destination that accepts the animal and arrange transport.

Know the limit: Do not delay evacuation to search for a missing animal.

Plan livestock relocation

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Owner
  • stock transport contact

Steps

  1. Record animal numbers, identification, handling needs and suitable destinations.
  2. Agree early movement triggers and transport arrangements.
  3. Check destination fencing, feed and reliable water ahead of time.

Know the limit: Inexperienced helpers should not load or handle large animals alone.

Check stock water from outside the pen

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Owner instructions
  • notebook

Steps

  1. Ask which troughs serve which animals and how to report a problem.
  2. Check visible water availability and note broken fittings from a safe position.
  3. Tell the owner promptly about loss of supply or contamination.

Know the limit: Do not enter with unfamiliar stock or adjust powered pumps.

  • NSW livestock water planning

Approach unfamiliar animals safely

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Owner guidance

Steps

  1. Ask the owner about behaviour, containment and handling rules.
  2. Observe from outside the enclosure and leave an escape path.
  3. Let an experienced handler manage feeding, restraint or transport.

Know the limit: Never approach a stressed, trapped, injured or protective animal.

Build an animal-care handover

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Written owner instructions

Steps

  1. Record routines, food amounts, water points and veterinary contacts.
  2. Agree access and who has authority for urgent veterinary decisions.
  3. Practise the routine with the owner before helping alone.

Know the limit: Only administer medication with clear instructions and appropriate ability.

Identify toxic or unsafe feed risks

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Feed labels
  • owner or vet advice

Steps

  1. Keep feeds labelled, dry and separated from chemicals.
  2. Ask the owner to show correct feed for each animal.
  3. Report mould, changed smell or accidental access before feeding.

Know the limit: Do not guess at feed substitutions or identify toxic plants from memory.

Plan animal welfare during drought

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Water budget
  • veterinarian or agricultural adviser

Steps

  1. Estimate available water and suitable feed with the owner.
  2. Review reserves regularly and arrange delivery early.
  3. Seek advice on welfare, stocking and relocation before a crisis.

Know the limit: Dams and tanks can become unsuitable even while water remains.

  • NSW livestock water planning

Know what to do with injured wildlife

Skill area: Animals & land. Level: Know your limits.

Have to hand

  • Wildlife rescue contact

Steps

  1. Stay clear of traffic and observe without handling.
  2. Record a precise location and call a wildlife rescue service.
  3. Keep pets away and follow the rescuer instructions.

Know the limit: Do not handle bats, snakes or large injured animals.
