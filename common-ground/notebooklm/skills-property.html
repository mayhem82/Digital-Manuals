Gates, sheds, boundaries and access.

Open and secure a country gate

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Owner demonstration
  • gloves

Steps

  1. Ask whether the gate must stay open or closed and note animals.
  2. Check hinges, latch, electric wires and the gate swing before moving it.
  3. Pass through and restore the agreed position securely.

Know the limit: Never assume a fence is unpowered or release animals onto a road.

Make a simple property map

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Paper or aerial image
  • owner input

Steps

  1. Mark address, gates, tracks, buildings and emergency access.
  2. Add water points, isolation locations and known hazards.
  3. Keep a dated copy in the emergency folder and share only by agreement.

Know the limit: Do not publish access codes or sensitive property details.

Inspect a fence from a safe distance

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Boots
  • phone
  • owner agreement

Steps

  1. Walk the agreed accessible route in daylight.
  2. Note broken wires, fallen posts, animal escapes and vegetation contact.
  3. Photograph from a safe position and report locations to the owner.

Know the limit: Electric and strained fencing can injure; repairs need the right training.

Keep emergency access clear

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Property plan
  • owner agreement

Steps

  1. Identify ambulance and fire vehicle access and turning points.
  2. Move agreed loose items and check gates are accessible.
  3. Have the owner arrange work on overgrowth, weak bridges or unsafe surfaces.

Know the limit: Do not widen tracks, cut trees or alter gates without permission.

Read a machinery safety label

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Machine manual
  • supervisor

Steps

  1. Identify the equipment and find the correct manual.
  2. Read hazard symbols, guards and exclusion zones.
  3. Arrange equipment-specific training before operating or clearing a blockage.

Know the limit: Never approach moving machinery, power take-offs or suspended loads.

  • SafeWork NSW farm safety

Spot shed storage hazards

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Torch
  • owner agreement

Steps

  1. Look from a safe accessible position for unstable stacks and blocked exits.
  2. Identify fuel, chemicals and batteries without opening containers.
  3. Agree which items need specialist storage or disposal advice.

Know the limit: Do not mix chemicals, disturb asbestos or enter unsafe sheds.

  • SafeWork NSW farm safety

Plan low-risk vegetation upkeep

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Property map
  • contractor contact

Steps

  1. Identify paths and access areas needing routine upkeep.
  2. Choose hand-level tasks within your skill and keep an escape route clear.
  3. Arrange qualified help for large trees, powerlines and machinery work.

Know the limit: Never clear during dangerous weather or work beneath hanging branches.

Make your address easy to find

Skill area: Country property. Level: Beginner friendly.

Have to hand

  • Visible address sign
  • property map

Steps

  1. Check the official address and entrance description are accurate.
  2. Make the number visible from the road with owner consent.
  3. Record gate directions and a safe meeting point for responders.

Know the limit: A map pin alone may send crews to the wrong gate.
