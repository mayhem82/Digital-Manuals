This venture introduces high-growth AgTech disruption to Kyūshū’s agricultural powerhouse. Fukuoka City is strategically selected as our operational launchpad due to its proactive, National Strategic Special Zone startup ecosystem, world-class international logistics infrastructure, and optimal regional climate to scale advanced agricultural solutions across Japan.

２．事業の概要 Business Outline
(1) 実施する事業の概要（商品・サービスの概要）
Provide an outline of your business (products and services provided).
Integrated Agricultural Value Chain (6th Industrialisation) and Ag-Tech Ecosystem
This venture introduces a complete 6th Industrialisation model to Fukuoka: cultivating premium Finger Limes (1st Industry), operating an advanced, non-heating food processing facility (2nd Industry), and executing strategic brand marketing for luxury domestic and export markets (3rd Industry). This is seamlessly integrated with a highly scalable Regenerative Ag-Tech division utilizing "Soil Food Web" microbiology to optimize crop yields and protect regional soil environments.

Phase 1: Market Entry & Cash Flow (Import & Processing Focus): Immediate launch of premium processed "Finger Lime Pearls," IQF frozen retail punnets, and freeze-dried citrus powders using Japan-certified imported fruit. All processing will take place in a specialized Fukuoka City facility adhering strictly to the Japanese Food Labelling Act and HACCP standards, targeting Michelin-starred restaurants, luxury hotels (such as the Ritz-Carlton Fukuoka), and high-end cocktail bars.

Phase 2: Local Cultivation & Genetic Proving (Production Focus): Establishing a local nursery footprint by importing elite, disease-free germplasm and stabilising it in controlled greenhouse facilities. Utilising optimised grafting methods on native Japanese rootstocks (e.g., Trifoliate Orange), we will build a stable, year-round supply of premium "Fukuoka-grown" Finger Limes.

Phase 3: Kyūshū-Wide Scaling & Ag-Tech Consultation (Collaboration Focus): Expanding our agricultural footprint via contract-grower agreements with local Fukuoka farmers. Concurrently, we will launch our Soil Food Web Diagnostic Laboratory, offering microscopic biomass testing and custom liquid bio-amendments. This helps local Kyūshū farmers transition regeneratively away from chemical fertilisers, directly supporting MAFF's national "Meidori" sustainable agriculture mandate.

Strategic Location & Logistical Advantages: Fukuoka City

The corporate headquarters, food processing facility, and scientific diagnostic lab will be established within Fukuoka City (Hakata-ku/Chuo-ku commercial zones).

Logistical Powerhouse: Proximity to Fukuoka Port and Fukuoka International Airport ensures seamless, cost-effective import logistics for Australian raw materials and rapid national distribution.

Market Access: Fukuoka City boasts one of Japan's highest densities of top-tier culinary establishments and luxury hospitality venues, providing an immediate, high-margin B2B consumer base right at our doorstep.

AgTech Innovation Hub: Operating inside Fukuoka City's National Strategic Special Zone gives the company direct access to bilingual startup support, local university research collaborations, and skilled food-science talent.

R01 — Year 2 salary unit

Flagged original: 4500 円

Appropriate proposed answer: Confirm whether the intended salary is ¥4,500,000. If confirmed, write 4,500 千円.

Reasoning: The form labels salary figures in thousands of yen; 4500 円 means ¥4,500.

OPEN — verification required

原文の「4500 円」は4,500円を意味します。意図する年額が450万円か確認し、確認後に「4,500 千円」と記載してください。様式の給与単位は千円です。

R02 — Supplier agreement

Flagged original: bulletproof

Appropriate proposed answer: Replace promotional certainty with a description of the actual agreement and attach executed contract.

Reasoning: No signed contract is established by the plan itself.

OPEN — verification required

「bulletproof」のような断定表現を避け、実際の契約条件を記載し、締結済み契約書を添付してください。計画書だけでは契約締結を確認できません。

R03 — Import clearance

Flagged original: pre-cleared

Appropriate proposed answer: Identify exact commodity and clearance documentation or qualify as subject to Japanese import approval.

Reasoning: Live propagation material and frozen fruit have different import pathways.

OPEN — verification required

対象品目と検疫・輸入許可の証拠を特定してください。証拠がない場合は日本の輸入承認を条件とする表現に修正してください。生きた繁殖材料と冷凍果実では要件が異なります。

R04 — Supply commitment

Flagged original: guaranteed supply

Appropriate proposed answer: Document contracted volumes, pricing and delivery terms, otherwise use conditional language.

Reasoning: The business plan alone does not demonstrate a binding guarantee.

OPEN — verification required

供給量、価格、納期などの契約条件を文書で示してください。拘束力のある保証が確認できない場合は条件付きの表現を使用してください。

R05 — Certification

Flagged original: Japan-certified

Appropriate proposed answer: Identify issuing authority, certificate number, product and validity, otherwise qualify.

Reasoning: Unidentified certification cannot be verified.

OPEN — verification required

認証機関、証明書番号、対象製品、有効期間を確認してください。証拠がなければ「日本認証済み」と断定しないでください。

R06 — Regulatory claim

Flagged original: absolute compliance

Appropriate proposed answer: Replace with demonstrable compliance processes and commodity-specific requirements.

Reasoning: Export experience is not proof of unconditional compliance.

OPEN — verification required

「絶対的な法令遵守」との表現は避け、対象品目ごとの具体的な適合手順と証拠を記載してください。過去の輸出実績だけでは全面的な適合を証明できません。

Flagged original: undisputed national capital

Appropriate proposed answer: Reframe as a proposed objective rather than an established market fact.

Reasoning: No independent market evidence supplied for this absolute claim.
