The manufacturer must build a purpose-designed transportable structural enclosure that delivers the specified 12.192 m clear internal longitudinal zone allocation (A 3.000 m, B 5.000 m, C 4.192 m), plus additional construction length for inter-zone partitions, end walls, frame and thermal interfaces. The nominal 2.350 m pre-insulation internal width and 2.690 m pre-insulation internal height are design targets. Exterior dimensions, mass, lifting points, transport permits, foundation and structural adequacy require engineering confirmation. 設備系統計画

電気

External protected supply → main isolator → RCD/RCBO distribution → dedicated 200V 60Hz appliance circuits and 100V lighting/data circuits. Separate protected feeds for plug-in freezers, future blast freezer, dishwasher, extraction and HVAC. Route conduits high along the service side; wet-area outlets must be splash-protected and above washdown exposure. Final breaker ratings and cable sizes require equipment nameplates and Japanese electrical design.

外部受電→主開閉器→漏電保護付き分電盤→200V/60Hz専用機器回路・100V照明／通信回路。冷凍庫、将来の急速冷凍機、食器洗浄機、換気・空調を分離。配線容量・遮断器容量は機器仕様確定後に設計。

給排水

Potable inlet at B service wall → backflow prevention → filtration (if required by water testing) → hot water supply → dedicated handwash and double sink. Washdown and sink wastes → removable solids basket → trapped sloped branch → sealed external wastewater outlet to an approved collection/sewer treatment system. No A-zone drain in Phase 1. Drain invert, slope and discharge permission remain to be engineered.

B区画側面から給水し、逆流防止、必要に応じたろ過、給湯を経て手洗い器と二槽シンクへ供給。排水は残渣捕集、トラップ、勾配付き配管を経て承認された排水設備へ。A区画は第1期排水口なし。

空調・換気

Filtered make-up air → B positive-pressure target relative to adjacent spaces, subject to hygienic airflow verification. Dedicated extract at B and C with external weatherproof discharge. Prevent extraction from defeating positive pressure. Future cold-room condenser provision outside; evaporator mount and sealed refrigerant penetration at A. Blast-freezer condenser heat must be rejected outdoors according to the selected unit's manufacturer.

ろ過給気によるB区画の相対陽圧を設計目標とし、B・Cの排気量と給気量を調整。将来の冷蔵室用屋外凝縮器、A区画内蒸発器支持、冷媒配管貫通部を準備。

衛生

Receiving → washing → extraction → separation → packaging → controlled storage. This logical product flow is not identical to a straight walk-through of the A–B–C arrangement; receiving and finished-goods routes need segregated scheduling, handling or barriers. Handwash is dedicated, not shared with food washing. Smooth cleanable coving, pest seals and documented sanitation are required.

原料受入→洗浄→抽出→選別→包装→温度管理保管。A–B–Cの直線配置だけでは一方向動線を保証できないため、原料・製品・人員の交差防止措置が必要。手洗い専用設備を設置。

構造・移設

Openings / 開口補強 Reinforced welded frames around B side entrance and B/C windows. Design weld details, corrosion protection and inspection hold points after structural analysis. JIS SS400-equivalent specification must be verified by mill certificates; do not assume existing ISO container steel is SS400.

開口補強 B区画側面入口とB・C区画の窓に溶接補強枠を設けます。溶接、防食及び検査方法は構造解析後に決定します。JIS SS400相当材は材料証明書で確認します。

Anchoring / 耐震固定 Engineer-designed foundation, corner-casting tie-downs and internal equipment restraints, considering site seismic demand, wind, uplift and relocation. Shock/vibration mounts must not compromise seismic restraint.

耐震固定 設置場所の地震・風荷重・浮き上がり・移設条件を考慮し、基礎、固定金具及び機器転倒防止を設計します。防振装置と耐震拘束の両立が必要です。

Floor / 床仕上げ Option 1: hygienic epoxy over a structurally stable prepared substrate. Option 2: welded-seam commercial sheet vinyl with coved edges for flexibility. Selection requires mobility frequency, drainage geometry and food authority acceptance.

床仕上げ 床仕上げ案1：衛生用エポキシ。案2：継ぎ目を溶接し巾木を立ち上げる業務用長尺塩ビシート。移設頻度、排水勾配及び保健所の判断により選定します。

Warehouse movement / 倉庫搬入 Measure clear door opening, canopy, slope, turning envelope, crane access, floor loading and overhead services. If opening is under 2.89 m, do not attempt entry: obtain an engineered handling route, consider exterior siting or an alternate building. Roof removal or height reduction is not an assumed safe method.

倉庫搬入 倉庫開口高さ、庇、傾斜、旋回範囲、クレーン作業域、床耐荷重、上空障害物を確認します。高さ不足の場合は無理に搬入せず、技術者による搬入計画を作成します。

第2期冷蔵室改修

Phase 1: 75 mm insulated A envelope, sealed door with observation window, IP65 lighting, pressure relief provision, conduits and commercial plug-in freezers. Phase 2: engineering review of insulation adequacy and vapour barrier, evaporator mounting, outdoor condenser, defrost water management, condensate drainage, controls, alarms and electrical demand. A Phase 1 no-drain arrangement does not by itself demonstrate Phase 2 drainage feasibility.

第1期は75mm断熱、点検窓付き断熱扉、IP65照明、圧力逃し口、配管予備経路と独立型冷凍庫。第2期は蒸発器、屋外凝縮器、除霜排水、制御・警報、電力容量、結露対策を再設計。第1期の排水口なし仕様では、第2期の排水成立は未証明です。

特注製作費の概算

Preliminary custom-build allowance: approximately ¥15 million–¥40 million for a purpose-manufactured structural shell and food-grade fit-out, insulated interiors, doors/windows, basic hygienic plumbing, electrical distribution, ventilation, structural reinforcement and installation-related fit-out. This is an unquoted, unverified conceptual estimate, not a verified Fukuoka contractor price, and excludes land, permits, major processing machinery, blast freezer, HPP system, freeze dryer, major cold-room plant, freight/import duties and abnormal site works. A highly equipped turnkey project may exceed this range.

特注製作の暫定概算：1,500万～4,000万円程度。特注構造体、断熱内装、開口部、基本給排水・電気・換気、構造補強等を想定した未見積の計画用参考値です。土地、許認可、主要加工機械、急速冷凍機、HPP装置、凍結乾燥機、冷蔵室設備、輸送・関税、特殊な現場工事は含みません。
