01 · Submission purpose

What this companion establishes

This companion organises the proposed venture, its source-derived representations, material inconsistencies, documentary dependencies and administrative decision points.

It is a review and supporting-document framework. It is not a replacement for Fukuoka City's prescribed Form 1-2, an immigration application, a regulatory permit, or proof that any recipient has assessed the proposal.

Evidence discipline. Statements in the original business plan are treated as applicant representations unless separately verified. A planned activity is not an approval; a prospective customer is not a contracted customer.
02 · Applicant's proposed enterprise

Business model and operating phases

Phase 1 — Imported fruit and processing

Proposed supply of Australian finger limes and manufacture or distribution of premium products including frozen fruit, finger lime pearls and freeze-dried citrus products, targeting hospitality and premium retail channels.

Phase 2 — Cultivation and nursery development

Proposed introduction of plant genetics, nursery establishment, greenhouse stabilisation and collaboration with Japanese growers. Import admissibility and phytosanitary conditions require product-specific confirmation.

Phase 3 — Soil microbiology and agricultural services

Proposed Soil Food Web diagnostic laboratory, biological soil testing, advisory services and liquid biological amendments. Applicable facility and product classifications remain to be verified.

Source: applicant's New Business Implementation Plan, Form 1-2, dated 2 October 2026. These are planned activities, not independently established operations.

03 · Document integrity

Material findings requiring disposition

F-001 · Year 2 salary unit inconsistency — demonstrated

The staffing section records Year 1 full-time salary as 4,000 千円, Year 2 as 4500 円, and Year 3 as 9,000 千円. The Year 2 unit is yen, not thousands of yen. Confirm the intended figure and amend the master.

F-002 · Capital and cash categories — reconciliation required

The plan uses multiple funding figures, including ¥30 million proposed capital and ¥40 million opening cash. Additional representations of available or secured funds require a dated sources-and-uses reconciliation; different categories must not be treated as inherently contradictory.

F-003 · Commercial and import assurances — verification required

Claims concerning an exclusive supplier relationship, pre-cleared genetics, certified imports and guaranteed supply need source documents. An export history does not itself establish approval for a particular live-plant import.

F-004 · Financial schedules — line-by-line reconciliation required

Previously identified differences between narrative financial projections and tabulated profit or cash movements require verification against the actual source schedules before correction.

F-005 · Prior audit arithmetic corrected

The Year 3 operating expense components total ¥27.5 million. A previous suggestion that they totalled ¥28 million was incorrect and is withdrawn.

F-006 · Repetition and certainty language

Repeated business-outline passages and absolute compliance language should be edited only after the applicant confirms the intended representations. Editorial changes must not silently alter facts or commitments.
