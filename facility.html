Dimensioned zones / 区画寸法

A · Cold storage / 冷蔵保管

Clear length 3.000 m; 75 mm insulation; clear width 2.200–2.230 m; ceiling clear height 2.615 m before floor and services. Plug-in freezers Phase 1; future cold room; no Phase 1 floor drain.

B · Processing / 加工

Clear length 5.000 m; 50 mm insulation; clear width 2.250–2.280 m; ceiling clear height 2.640 m before floor and services. Side entrance, fixed window, handwash, double sink, wet-process drain and filtered positive-pressure supply.

C · Packaging / office / 包装・事務

Clear length 4.192 m; 50 mm insulation; clear width 2.250–2.280 m; ceiling clear height 2.640 m before floor and services. Fixed window, insulated inner door behind double doors.

Reference discrepancy to resolve: Paul's original illustration marks a 1.70 m width, whereas the later C08 engineering design basis specifies 2.350–2.380 m pre-insulation internal width. The 1.70 m label is not silently adopted as the fabrication width. This conflict remains OPEN for Paul's decision.

Pre-insulation internal design width 2.350–2.380 m; height 2.690 m. External shell to be engineered to preserve required internal dimensions.

Utilities / 設備

Electrical / 電気

Western Japan 60 Hz. Dedicated 200 V appliance circuits; 100 V lighting and office; RCD/RCBO, grounding, protected external inlet and future refrigeration allowances. Final ratings depend on equipment specifications.

Water and drainage / 給排水

Potable supply, backflow prevention, filtered supply if needed, heated dedicated handwash, double sink, solids basket, trapped graded drain, sealed external wastewater outlet. No Phase 1 drain in A.

Airflow / 換気

Filtered make-up air and positive-pressure target in B; coordinated B/C extraction, outdoor condenser and future evaporator provisions. Airflow balance requires professional verification.

Food flow / 衛生動線

Receiving → washing → extraction → separation → packaging → controlled storage. A–B–C geometry alone does not prove segregated one-way HACCP handling.

Construction and mobility / 構造・移設

JIS SS400-equivalent certified steel; reinforced welded frames at openings; engineered seismic and wind restraints; transport/lifting provisions; hygienic epoxy or welded vinyl flooring subject to movement and approval; warehouse door, load, turning and cranage survey.

Phase 2 cold room requires vapour barrier, condenser, evaporator, defrost drainage, alarms and electrical re-evaluation. Phase 1 no-drain arrangement does not establish Phase 2 feasibility.

Regulatory and fabrication gates / 許認可・製作条件

OPEN: Fukuoka food-business permission/notification, HACCP-based operational procedures, building/fire/zoning status, electrical and wastewater acceptance, site-specific structural/seismic design, manufacturer drawings, inspection plan and commissioning.

Concept only. No Japanese regulatory approval, engineering certification or fabrication-ready drawing has been issued.

Unquoted planning allowance: ¥15–40 million for a custom structural shell and food-grade fit-out, excluding major machinery, permits, land, exceptional site work and freight. Not a contractor quotation.
