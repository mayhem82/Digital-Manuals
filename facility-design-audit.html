Utilities coordination / 設備系統計画

Electrical / 電気

External protected supply → main isolator → RCD/RCBO distribution → dedicated 200V 60Hz appliance circuits and 100V lighting/data circuits. Separate protected feeds for plug-in freezers, future blast freezer, dishwasher, extraction and HVAC. Route conduits high along the service side; wet-area outlets must be splash-protected and above washdown exposure. Final breaker ratings and cable sizes require equipment nameplates and Japanese electrical design.

外部受電→主開閉器→漏電保護付き分電盤→200V/60Hz専用機器回路・100V照明／通信回路。冷凍庫、将来の急速冷凍機、食器洗浄機、換気・空調を分離。配線容量・遮断器容量は機器仕様確定後に設計。

Water & drainage / 給排水

Potable inlet at B service wall → backflow prevention → filtration (if required by water testing) → hot water supply → dedicated handwash and double sink. Washdown and sink wastes → removable solids basket → trapped sloped branch → sealed external wastewater outlet to an approved collection/sewer treatment system. No A-zone drain in Phase 1. Drain invert, slope and discharge permission remain to be engineered.

B区画側面から給水し、逆流防止、必要に応じたろ過、給湯を経て手洗い器と二槽シンクへ供給。排水は残渣捕集、トラップ、勾配付き配管を経て承認された排水設備へ。A区画は第1期排水口なし。

HVAC / 空調・換気

Filtered make-up air → B positive-pressure target relative to adjacent spaces, subject to hygienic airflow verification. Dedicated extract at B and C with external weatherproof discharge. Prevent extraction from defeating positive pressure. Future cold-room condenser provision outside; evaporator mount and sealed refrigerant penetration at A. Blast-freezer condenser heat must be rejected outdoors according to the selected unit's manufacturer.

ろ過給気によるB区画の相対陽圧を設計目標とし、B・Cの排気量と給気量を調整。将来の冷蔵室用屋外凝縮器、A区画内蒸発器支持、冷媒配管貫通部を準備。

Hygiene / 衛生

Receiving → washing → extraction → separation → packaging → controlled storage. This logical product flow is not identical to a straight walk-through of the A–B–C arrangement; receiving and finished-goods routes need segregated scheduling, handling or barriers. Handwash is dedicated, not shared with food washing. Smooth cleanable coving, pest seals and documented sanitation are required.

原料受入→洗浄→抽出→選別→包装→温度管理保管。A–B–Cの直線配置だけでは一方向動線を保証できないため、原料・製品・人員の交差防止措置が必要。手洗い専用設備を設置。

Construction & mobility / 構造・移設

Openings / 開口補強Reinforced welded frames around B side entrance and B/C windows. Design weld details, corrosion protection and inspection hold points after structural analysis. JIS SS400-equivalent specification must be verified by mill certificates; do not assume existing ISO container steel is SS400.
Anchoring / 耐震固定Engineer-designed foundation, corner-casting tie-downs and internal equipment restraints, considering site seismic demand, wind, uplift and relocation. Shock/vibration mounts must not compromise seismic restraint.
Floor / 床仕上げOption 1: hygienic epoxy over a structurally stable prepared substrate. Option 2: welded-seam commercial sheet vinyl with coved edges for flexibility. Selection requires mobility frequency, drainage geometry and food authority acceptance.
Warehouse movement / 倉庫搬入Measure clear door opening, canopy, slope, turning envelope, crane access, floor loading and overhead services. If opening is under 2.89 m, do not attempt entry: obtain an engineered handling route, consider exterior siting or an alternate building. Roof removal or height reduction is not an assumed safe method.

Phase 2 cold-room conversion / 第2期冷蔵室改修

Phase 1: 75 mm insulated A envelope, sealed door with observation window, IP65 lighting, pressure relief provision, conduits and commercial plug-in freezers. Phase 2: engineering review of insulation adequacy and vapour barrier, evaporator mounting, outdoor condenser, defrost water management, condensate drainage, controls, alarms and electrical demand. A Phase 1 no-drain arrangement does not by itself demonstrate Phase 2 drainage feasibility.

第1期は75mm断熱、点検窓付き断熱扉、IP65照明、圧力逃し口、配管予備経路と独立型冷凍庫。第2期は蒸発器、屋外凝縮器、除霜排水、制御・警報、電力容量、結露対策を再設計。第1期の排水口なし仕様では、第2期の排水成立は未証明です。
